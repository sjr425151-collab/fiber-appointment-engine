01The company

About Dynamic Construction MI

Since 2008, a family-owned Carleton builder trusted across Monroe County for pole barns, roofs, and the details in between.

We build post-frame pole barns and garages, replace and repair roofs, restore aging roofs with GACO silicone systems, and install seamless gutters, for homes, farms and businesses across Southeast Michigan.

05Roofing expertise

A roof is a system, not a patch.

Decking, underlayment, shingles, flashing and ventilation all have to work together. Our roofing work is built around that, with the credentials to back it up.

  1. 01

    Owens Corning Preferred Contractor

    An Owens Corning Preferred Contractor installing Owens Corning roofing systems.

  2. 02

    50-year manufacturer material warranty on qualifying roofs

    Qualifying Owens Corning roof systems carry a 50-year manufacturer material warranty, alongside the company's own workmanship warranty.

  3. 03

    Most roof replacements completed in one day

    Most residential roof replacements are completed in a single day, weather permitting. Larger or complex roofs take longer, and you get a timeline before work begins.

  4. 04

    GACO Certified Installer

    A GACO Certified Installer, trained and approved to install GACO silicone roof systems to manufacturer standards.

  5. 05

    Lomanco Ventilation certified

    Lomanco Ventilation certified, so gutters and roof ventilation are planned as one system.

  6. 06

    Thorough clean-up

    Tarps protect landscaping during tear-off, and magnetic sweepers collect nails and debris before the crew leaves.
