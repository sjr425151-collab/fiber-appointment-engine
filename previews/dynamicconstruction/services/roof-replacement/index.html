Roof Replacement in Monroe County & Southeast Michigan

Aging materials, storm damage and repeated leaks eventually make repairs a poor investment. A full replacement resets the clock with new decking where needed, fresh underlayment, proper ventilation and a new shingle system.

Most of our roof replacements in Monroe, Newport, Temperance and the surrounding area are completed in a single day, weather permitting, so your household is disrupted as little as possible.

05Questions

Roof Replacement FAQ

How do I know if my roof needs replacing or just repairing?

Missing or curled shingles, leaks, water stains inside, or shingle granules in the gutters are common warning signs. A free inspection tells you whether a repair will solve the problem or whether replacement is the smarter investment.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most roof replacements are completed in one day, weather permitting. Larger or more complex projects may take longer, and you'll get a clear timeline before work begins.

How much will my new roof cost?

Cost depends on roof size, pitch, materials and any repairs to the structure underneath. You'll receive a detailed written estimate so you know exactly what to expect.

What warranty comes with a new roof?

Qualifying installations include the Owens Corning 50-year manufacturer material warranty, plus the company's own workmanship warranty. Ask for the specific terms that apply to your roof.

Will replacing my roof make a mess of my yard?

Tarps protect your property during tear-off, and magnetic sweepers collect nails and debris before the crew leaves.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. Permits and required inspections are handled for you according to local building codes.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, financing options are available and can be reviewed during your estimate.
