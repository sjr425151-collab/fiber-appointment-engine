01Overview

Roof Repair in Monroe County & Southeast Michigan

A small leak can quietly cause rot, mold, and damage to ceilings and walls. Catching it early is almost always cheaper than waiting.

We inspect, find where the water is actually getting in, and recommend an honest fix, whether that's a targeted repair or, when it makes more sense, a replacement.

02Why it matters

What you get with roof repair from Dynamic Construction

  • Know the warning signs

  • Repair or replace, honestly

05Questions

Roof Repair FAQ

What are the signs I need a roof repair?

Water stains on ceilings, missing or curled shingles, sagging areas, damaged flashing, and shingle granules collecting in gutters are the most common signs.

Do you offer emergency roof service?

Emergency service is available. If water is coming in, call (734) 636-3736.

Should I repair or replace my roof?

It depends on the roof's age, the extent of the damage, and whether problems keep recurring. A free inspection gives you an honest answer.
