01Overview

Roof Restoration & Coatings in Monroe County & Southeast Michigan

When a flat, metal or rubber roof is aging but still structurally sound, replacement isn't always the only answer. A silicone coating system can restore and protect it, often delaying the cost of a full replacement.

As a GACO Certified Installer, our team has completed manufacturer training and is approved to install GACO roofing systems to manufacturer standards.

02Why it matters

What you get with roof restoration from Dynamic Construction

  • Seamless leak protection

    Silicone coatings create a continuous waterproof membrane designed to protect against leaks and weather damage.

  • Performs in standing water

    Unlike many traditional coatings, GACO silicone systems are designed to keep their adhesion and performance where water sits on the roof.

  • Manufacturer-backed options

    Certified installers may offer access to manufacturer-supported warranty programs on qualifying projects.

05Questions

Roof Restoration FAQ

Is a roof coating right for my building?

If your roof is aging, leaking or worn but still structurally sound, a coating system may be a cost-effective alternative to replacement. An inspection will confirm whether it's a good fit.

What types of roofs can be coated?

GACO products are commonly used on flat, metal and rubber roofs, including commercial and industrial buildings.

Why does GACO certification matter?

Certified installers have received manufacturer-approved training and support, which helps ensure the system is installed correctly and performs as intended.
