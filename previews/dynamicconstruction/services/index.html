Services

Everything above the foundation, built to last.

Post-frame buildings, roofing and exterior work for homes, farms and businesses across Monroe County and Southeast Michigan.

01 / Buildings

Buildings

Post-frame pole barns, garages and workshops planned around your property.

  • Pole Barn Construction

    Custom post-frame buildings for storage, equipment, agriculture and work.

  • Garage Construction

    Detached, garage-style post-frame buildings for vehicles, tools and projects.

02 / Roofing

Roofing

Replacement, repair and restoration from an Owens Corning Preferred Contractor.

  • Residential Roofing

    Shingle, metal and steel roofing for homes and businesses across Monroe County.

  • Roof Replacement

    Complete tear-off and replacement, usually finished in a single day.

  • Roof Repair

    Leak, storm-damage and flashing repairs, with emergency service available.

  • Roof Restoration & Coatings

    GACO silicone coatings that waterproof and extend the life of existing roofs.
