About

A family business that builds like it’s our own property.

Established in 2008 in Carleton, Michigan. Pole barns, roofs and exterior work for homes, farms and businesses across Monroe County.

01Our story

Established
2008
Based in
Carleton, Michigan

No pole barn job too big or too small.

Family owned and operated, Dynamic Construction MI has served residential and commercial clients throughout Carleton and neighboring communities since 2008, from roof repairs and replacements to complete post-frame buildings: pole barns, garages, and agricultural and equipment buildings.

The team brings decades of roofing experience across metal roofs, wood shake roofs and asphalt shingle installation, alongside pole barn construction of every size. We’re fully licensed and insured for your protection.

Today we build and roof across Monroe, Wayne, Lenawee and Oakland counties, with the same approach on every job: dependable work, clear communication, and results built to last.

  • 20+ years of experience
  • Licensed and insured
  • Owens Corning Preferred Contractor
  • GACO Certified Installer
  • Lomanco Ventilation certified
  • Financing available
